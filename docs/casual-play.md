# Casual play model

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/casual.ts 5 --write`. Spec target (docs/concept.md 17.1): a casual player with a few short sessions per day and offline earnings reaches the end of Season 1 in about 7 to 12 days.

Model: each session lasts 5 minutes of optimal play (balance bot), the player is away the rest of the day; on return the offline dialog pays 50% of income for the time away, capped at 8 h (no ad doubling assumed; doubling would shorten this).

| Sessions per day | Gap between sessions | Days to everything maxed | Total active play |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | every 24.0 h | 19.0 days | 1.7 h |
| 2 | every 12.0 h | 9.5 days | 1.7 h |
| 3 | every 8.0 h | 6.3 days | 1.7 h |
| 5 | every 4.8 h | 4.2 days | 1.8 h |
| 8 | every 3.0 h | 3.1 days | 2.2 h |

Reading: 2 to 3 sessions a day lands at 6 to 10 days, inside the target. One session a day is slow because the 8 h cap wastes most of a 24 h gap; 5 to 8 sessions a day approaches the pure active-play time. Offline doubling by rewarded ad and the ski bus are not modelled, so real players should be a little faster.
